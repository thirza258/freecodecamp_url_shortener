1. Overview & Local Data Boundary

At {{ SITE_NAME }} ({{ SHORT_LINK_DOMAIN }}), we believe in link simplicity and complete privacy. Unlike commercial link shorteners that monetize user traffic, our service is completely self-hosted on private servers. All click logs, destination URLs, and analytics stay strictly within our isolated database.

2. Information We Process

  • Destination URLs: Long URLs that you submit to generate short links are stored in our local PostgreSQL database to execute redirects.
  • Account Information: If you create a free account to manage links, we store your username and salted password hash. We never store plain passwords.
  • Routing Telemetry & Analytics: When a shortened link is clicked, our server records aggregate counts (timestamp, referer header, user-agent) strictly to provide link owners with aggregate click analytics.
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: IP addresses may be temporarily evaluated in local Redis caches solely for rate limiting and DDoS mitigation.

3. Artificial Intelligence Policy

{{ SITE_NAME }} does not utilize artificial intelligence inference or external AI APIs. We do not dispatch any prompt, URL, or visitor data to external AI models or services like OpenRouter. All redirection and tracking logic operates strictly via local, deterministic server code.

4. Cookies and Session Management

We only use essential HTTP cookies necessary for session authentication when you log into your dashboard. We do not use third-party cross-site trackers, behavioral ad pixels, or surveillance cookies.

5. Data Retention & User Controls

You maintain full control of your shortened links. Authenticated users can review, inspect, and purge shortened links directly through the Dashboard at any time.

6. Security Measures

All connections are enforced over TLS (HTTPS). Database access is restricted to localhost network boundaries with strong credential isolation.
