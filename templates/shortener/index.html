[ QUESTIONS & ANSWERS ]

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our URL shortener, slug policies, or analytics? Here are the answers.

Are shortened links permanent? +
Yes, all short links created on {{ SITE_NAME }} remain active indefinitely as long as they comply with our acceptable use policies and security standards.
Do I need an account to shorten URLs? +
No account is required to generate short URLs. You can paste and shorten instantly as a guest. Creating a free account lets you view and manage your full link portfolio and access personal click history in your private dashboard.
Can I specify a custom slug? +
Yes! You can enter any custom alphanumeric slug (e.g. my-campaign or portfolio). If the slug is already taken or is a reserved system keyword, the form will guide you to select an alternative.
What analytics and metrics are tracked for each link? +
For each short link, we track total redirect counts, the exact timestamp of the most recent redirect, user-agent details, and referer sources, allowing you to monitor audience engagement in real time.
How does the QR code generator work? +
Every time a short link is created, a high-resolution QR code is automatically generated. You can download the QR image directly from the success page or scan it instantly with any smartphone camera.
What rate limits are enforced? +
To maintain high reliability and prevent abusive spam, link creation is capped per IP address. Redirect traffic is heavily cached and optimized for high concurrent throughput.
